FAQS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about station capacity, pricing, and games.

FAQ // 01

DO I NEED AN ADVANCE BOOKING?

Pre-booking is recommended to reserve your console station and preferred time slot. Walk-ins are also welcome between 10:00 AM and 09:30 PM. Call or message 99944 20447 to book in advance.

FAQ // 02

HOW MANY PLAYERS PER PS5?

Both stations accommodate up to 4 players per console with seating and multiple controllers for local multiplayer.

FAQ // 03

WHAT ARE THE HOURLY RATES?

Weekday rates: ₹148 for 1 Hour, ₹288 for 2 Hours. Weekend rates: ₹198 for 1 Hour, ₹348 for 2 Hours. Extra controllers at ₹50. Value combo packages available.

FAQ // 04

ARE ALL GAMES INCLUDED?

Yes. Full access to our entire installed library (FC 26, WWE 2K26, Cricket 24, Uncharted, Mortal Kombat 1, GTA V, Spider-Man 2, RDR2, and co-op games) with no extra fees.

FAQ // 05

IS THE LOUNGE AIR CONDITIONED?

Yes. The entire lounge maintains a temperature-controlled, quiet, air-conditioned climate for comfortable gaming.

FAQ // 06

WHERE IS THE LOUNGE LOCATED?

Located on the main road at Thoppampatti Pirivu, Coimbatore - 641017 (CBE - 17), directly opposite HDFC Bank. Easy parking available.
